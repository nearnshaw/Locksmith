# H1-01 · Funny, not frustrating

- **IF/THEN:** IF every attempt (padlock click, E to shake, clicking a prop) answers within half a second with a new deadpan line and the level's danger visibly advances, THEN a first-time player plays all three levels (about 5 minutes) to the end card without quitting, and describes the game as funny rather than frustrating.
- **Source section:** §3 Core Loop — "Why repetition 10 remains appealing"
- **Cheapest killing test:** desktop Explorer, the greybox in this repo, owner self-test first (kill-check: did you laugh at least once per level, and did you want to quit?); then 3 first-time players watched with a stopwatch.
- **Key metric:** (agent-proposed, owner to confirm) all 3 levels completed, and the tester's one-word description is on the "funny" side; failure: quitting before the end card, or "frustrating / boring / confusing".
