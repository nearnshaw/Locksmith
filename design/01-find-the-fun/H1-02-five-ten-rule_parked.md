# H1-02 · The 5/10 rule on the padlock

- **IF/THEN:** IF the first frame after the tutorial shows the glowing padlock with the prompt "Use lockpick" and the HUD reads "Lockpicks: 0", THEN at least 80% of first-time testers click the padlock or press E within 5 seconds and can say the goal ("get out of the chair") within 10 seconds, without help.
- **Source section:** §2 First Minutes & How to Play

- **Key metric:** program bar (5/10 rule): share of testers acting by 5 s and stating the goal by 10 s; failure: below 80%.
