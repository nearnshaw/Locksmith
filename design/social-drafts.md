**Title:** I made a Decentraland escape game where escaping is not an option

**Body:**
Locksmith is a 7-minute parody of escape games. You play as your own avatar, "the most famous escape artist alive", chained to a chair with no lockpicks. There's a skill screen, a lockpicking tutorial, a cell, a conveyor belt into lava and a shark tank. You can try everything, and the game has a reply for every attempt.

It's built with SDK7: custom seated emote, cinematic camera shots, and a lot of deadpan writing. Link: [LINK]
