# Player feedback — design groundwork (2026-10-23)

Prepared under the player-feedback reference of the design skills. Design input only: no survey is installed or collecting anything; implementing it is a separate, owner-authorised step with the scene-feedback skill.

**Iteration:** pass 9 build (three rooms, thugs, vault door, scream ending).

**Decision this feedback informs:** does the no-agency barrier read as a joke the player is in on, or as a game ignoring them (hypothesis H1-01)? Secondary: are the levels too long once the lines stop surprising?

**Proposed questions, in the player's words (asked once, on the end card):**
1. "Did the ending make you laugh, smile, or neither?" (laugh / smile / neither)
2. "Was there a moment you wanted to quit? Which room?" (none / the cell / the belt / the tank)
3. "Which of these did you try? Click the padlock · press E · click things in the room · talk to the thugs" (multi-select)

**Who can answer and when:** anyone who reached the end card (questions 1 and 2); question 3 also fits a player who quit early, so it could be asked on scene exit, but asking only at the end keeps it to one moment. An unsuccessful attempt is the whole game, so no filter is needed.

**Recommendation:** one question set on the end card, three questions, no free text; this answers H1-01's kill-check without interrupting the joke. Wording follows the scene-feedback module's question guide when implemented.
