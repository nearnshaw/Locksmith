# Locksmith: footage shot list

What to record in the Explorer for the footage posts and the Shorts, and how.

## Tips

- Required: one clean in-world 16:9 screenshot, 1920x1080 or larger, with no UI and no text, just gameplay with people playing.
- Recommended: 2-4 more 16:9 gameplay stills, at least 1920x1080, for social posts, the newsletter and press.
- Optional trailer: under 30 s, 16:9 1920x1080, with people in motion in the first three seconds.
- Trailer vertical cut: 9:16 1080x1920, under 30 s, for mobile and social.
- Trailer captions or on-screen text so it works with the sound off.
- Record in the Decentraland desktop Explorer at 1920x1080 or higher, in the default third-person camera, with the client UI hidden where you can.
- Use only your own avatar in public clips; the game hides other visitors at the chair, but keep anyone else's name out of frame.
- Record with sound on: the chain rattle, the lock click and the countdown music carry the clips. The music is "Spy Glass" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC BY 4.0: credit it in any post that uses it.
- Each level runs about two minutes; the conveyor and the shark shots happen near the end of theirs, so start recording a little before.
- For vertical reels, keep the chair in the middle third of a 16:9 recording so a 9:16 crop keeps it.
- Keep the endings out of public clips: what happens when the belt reaches the end, what happens when the shark reaches you, and the end card.

## Shots

### Footage: standard procedure  (`footage-01`)

From the title screen: press Start, press All in on Lockpicking so it reads 12, skip through the tutorial. Cut to the cell in the default third-person camera: click the padlock on your right hip and hold on the thought "You reach for your lockpick. You have 0 lockpicks." 12 to 15 seconds. Do not show the later rooms.

Caption: Right. Standard procedure.  [FOOTAGE]

### Footage: shaking the chains  (`footage-02`)

In the cell, third person, the chair in the middle of the frame. Press E two or three times: the seated struggle, the chain rattle, the thought box, a thug reacting in his speech bubble. Sound on. 8 to 10 seconds. Works as a vertical reel and loops.

Caption: The chains are rated for 20 kN. You are rated for 0 lockpicks.  [FOOTAGE]

### Footage: Conveyor 3  (`footage-03`)

Conveyor 3, late in the level: the belt carrying the chair, then the game's own cinematic angle past the lava as the chair creeps toward it. Cut before the pallet reaches the end of the belt; what happens there stays secret. 8 to 12 seconds.

Caption: Destination: lava.  [FOOTAGE]

### Footage: the release  (`footage-04`)

Tank room: the board hits SHARK RELEASE IN 0:00, the vault door swings open, the game cuts to its low angle beside the gate and the shark lines up and swims out nose first. Cut as it turns down the room toward the camera. Never show it reaching the chair. About 10 seconds.

Caption: The shark's status remains: hopeful.  [FOOTAGE]

### Footage: 30-second trailer  (`footage-05`)

Trailer cut, under 30 seconds, horizontal 16:9 with a vertical 9:16 version. 0-3 s: shaking the chains (motion in the first three seconds). 3-7 s: the skill screen, All in on Lockpicking. 7-11 s: clicking the padlock, "You have 0 lockpicks." 11-16 s: the spoon exchange in the thugs' bubbles. 16-21 s: the belt and the lava angle. 21-27 s: the vault door opening and the shark coming out. 27-30 s: LOCKSMITH wordmark. Captions on. No endings, no end card.

Caption: LOCKSMITH. Thirty seconds of not escaping.  [FOOTAGE]
